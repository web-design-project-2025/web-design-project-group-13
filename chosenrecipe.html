Ingredients

Water 2 liters
Vegetable broth 4 dice
Banana shallots 3
Fresh green asparagus 500 g
Butter at room temperature 75 g
Arborioris 4 dl
Dry white wine or 1 tablespoon white wine vinegar 2 dl
Grated Parmesan cheese 3 dl
Salt 1 pinch
Coarsely ground black pepper 1 tsp
Lemon juice 2 tbsp

Step By Step

Bring water and broth to a boil. Keep warm.
Peel and finely chop the onion. Trim the asparagus and cut off the bottom part if it is coarse. Cut off the buds and set aside. Cut the stem into thin slices.
Heat 25g butter in a wide saucepan. Sweat the onion until it becomes soft, without it taking color. Add the finely chopped asparagus and fry for about 3 minutes.
Add the rice and sauté for another 3 minutes, stirring constantly. When the rice is shiny and translucent, pour in the wine and let it boil for 2 minutes.
Dilute the risotto with hot broth, stirring constantly. The risotto is ready when the rice feels creamy but still has a firm core.
Fry the asparagus buds in a hot frying pan with 1 tablespoon butter. Salt.
Stir in the remaining butter and finely grated Parmesan cheese into the risotto. Remove from heat and season with salt, freshly ground black pepper and lemon juice.
Serve the asparagus risotto on flat plates. Top with freshly fried asparagus buds.
