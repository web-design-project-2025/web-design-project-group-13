About us

At Lettuce Cook, we believe that cooking should be more about joy than stress. In a world flooded with food blogs,
diet trends, and endless scrolling for the “perfect” dinner idea, we set out with a simple mission: to help real people
find recipes that actually fit their lives.

Whether you're cooking on a budget, dealing with food allergies, trying to impress someone, or just making a midnight
snack with what's left in your fridge — we've been there. That's why Lettuce Cook is more than just a recipe site.
We're your cooking companion, a digital kitchen buddy who doesn't judge your cravings or your cupboard.

Our recipe platform was built with flexibility and simplicity in mind. You can filter out ingredients you dislike, discover meals based on what you have, and finally answer the eternal question: What should I cook today? We make sure
our recipes are easy to follow, taste-tested, and designed to make your cooking experience smoother, smarter, and
way more fun.

Meet the Team

Emilia is the creative spark behind Lettuce Cook. A lifelong foodie and design lover, she makes sure everything you
see and interact with feels delightful and intuitive. She's all about aesthetics — but also deeply cares that our recipes are accessible, inclusive, and empowering. Emilia is the reason Lettuce Cook looks and feels like a place you'd want
to spend time in, not just another recipe dump.

Edvin is our resident code chef — the guy who made it all work behind the scenes. With a background in full-stack development and an unhealthy obsession with spicy ramen, Edvin built the engine that powers our site. Thanks to
him, filtering out cilantro (or whatever your food nemesis is) feels like magic. He's also the one who can turn even
the wildest idea into a working feature.

Sehr brings the flavor and the flow. As our content lead and culinary curator, she's the voice you hear in every recipe, blog post, and tip. Sehr knows that food isn't just about what we eat — it's about how we live, connect, and unwind.
She makes sure every recipe we share is not only delicious but has a story behind it. Oh, and she makes the best playlists for cooking — trust us.

Together, we're Lettuce Cook — and we're here to help you fall in love with cooking, one bite at a time.
